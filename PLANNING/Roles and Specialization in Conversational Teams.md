# Roles and Specialization in Conversational Teams

### Writer

Generates content—drafts, summaries, reports optimized for clarity and style.

### **Critic**

Reviews outputs against rubrics—identifies gaps, errors, and improvement opportunities.

### **Researcher**

Retrieves information—searches databases, web, documents; synthesizes findings.

### **Toolsmith**

Executes technical operations—code, calculations, data transformations.

### **Judge**

Evaluates quality—scores outputs on defined dimensions with rationale.
